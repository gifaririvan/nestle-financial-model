## Limitations

This is an illustrative, publicly sourced model built for portfolio purposes. The main simplifications are:

**Scope and definitions**
- Annual, consolidated model in nominal CHF; no segment, regional, price/volume or seasonal detail.
- EBITDA is reported EBIT + D&A, so it includes restructuring-type items in other trading and operating expenses.
- Net debt is financial debt plus revolver less cash (short-term investments not deducted). Leverage ratios are therefore higher than company-reported "underlying" metrics and not directly comparable.
- Ratios use year-end balances rather than averages.

**Scenario design**
- 17 of 35 drivers vary by scenario: the 10 operating drivers plus working-capital days, capex, target payout, planned buybacks and the associates dividend rate.
- Tax rate, interest rates, the debt path, D&A rates, intangible capex, prepayment and accrual days, the NCI share and the non-cash share of other expenses are common to all scenarios.
- Scenarios are illustrative and not probability-weighted.

**Financing**
- Total financial debt follows a fixed % path and refinancing is assumed to be available; the revolving credit line has no size limit or covenants.
- Interest is charged at flat rates on opening balances (circularity switch off by default).
- There is no fixed/floating mix, maturity schedule or modelling of other financial items (e.g., pension interest, FX results).

**Shareholder returns**
- Dividends follow a simple rule: target payout, floored at the prior-year CHF dividend and capped at 100% of profit.
- Buybacks are all-or-nothing against the leverage ceiling.
- No share count, EPS or DPS is modelled.

**Tax**
- A single effective tax rate (23.5%) applies to all forecast years and scenarios.
- Cash taxes come from a simplified tax-depreciation schedule whose opening tax basis is proxied to book value. Only deferred tax liabilities move with this timing difference; other deferred tax balances are held flat.

**Balance-sheet items held constant**
- Short-term investments, financial assets, derivatives, provisions, employee benefits, current tax balances and held-for-sale items are held flat.
- No acquisitions or disposals are assumed.
- Impairments are proxied as a fixed 35% non-cash share of other trading and operating expenses, written off against goodwill rather than tested asset by asset.
- Currency retranslation is set to zero, so the translation reserve is flat, although FX has historically been material for a CHF reporter.

**Operating mechanics**
- COGS is driven by a YoY growth rate rather than a % of sales, so gross margin is an output.
- Capex is a % of sales and D&A a blended rate on opening net book value, with no asset-life or IFRS 16 lease detail.
- Income from associates grows at an assumed rate independent of the investment balance; dividends from associates are a fixed % of the opening balance.
